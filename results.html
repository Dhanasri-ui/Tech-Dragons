Termination Restrictions

Example finding · Cancellation terms

HIGH PRIORITY
TERMINATION
Illustrative contract evidence
“The agreement may not be terminated before the end of the initial term.”
Example page reference: Page 4 · Clause 8.2

Why review this? This wording could restrict early cancellation. Check whether exceptions, notice periods, and termination rights are clearly explained elsewhere in the agreement.

Suggested review: Check whether either party can terminate for breach, non-performance, or other agreed circumstances.
